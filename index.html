Jumper limitations used by this app

These are conservative generic engineering assumptions, not manufacturer-confirmed Secfetronik hardware rules.

Internal bridgeAllowed only between adjacent terminals on the same side of the stack: odd-to-odd or even-to-even with a difference of 2 (for example 1↔3 or 2↔4).
External wireAny two distinct accessible terminals can be selected in principle, but electrical suitability is not guaranteed.
Same contact pairAn external wire across 1↔2, 3↔4, etc. is allowed only with a warning because it permanently bypasses that switching contact. It is blocked as an internal bridge.
Multi-terminal commonIf a terminal already participates in another jumper, the app warns that a common bus/branch is being created and combined current must be checked.
Not electrically validatedThe app does not know L1/L2/L3, circuit voltage, load, fault current, conductor size, or protective devices, so a physically allowed jumper may still be electrically unsafe.
Manufacturer confirmationRigid internal bridge availability, spacing, insulation, current rating, and layer restrictions must be confirmed for the actual switch series.
